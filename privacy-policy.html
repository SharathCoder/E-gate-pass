Privacy Policy

Last Updated: JUNE 2026

1. Data Collected

SmartGate Pass collects the following information to facilitate campus security:

  • Student Name, USN, Department, and Hostel Room Number
  • Phone Number and Guardian Contact Information (if applicable)
  • Cryptographic Entry & Exit Records (Timestamps)
  • Location Verification Data (GPS Geofencing at time of pass generation)

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Data is strictly utilized for Campus Security, Student Verification, Entry & Exit Monitoring, and Emergency Contact Management.

3. Data Security

All data is securely stored utilizing Google Firebase enterprise-grade infrastructure. Access is strictly restricted to authorized administrators and security personnel using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).

4. Student Rights

Students retain the right to contact the administration regarding data corrections, account issues, or access requests via the Support Portal.
