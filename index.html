Important Instructions

A QR Pass will NOT be generated under the following conditions:

1. Invalid USN

  • Please enter a valid and registered USN.

2. No Internet Connection

  • Check your internet connection and reload the page.

3. Location Access Disabled

  • Enable location services on your device.
  • Ensure your browser is allowed to access location in Site Settings.
  • QR Pass generation is allowed only when you are within approximately 850 meters of the campus.

4. Active QR Pass Already Exists

  • Only one QR Pass can be active at a time.
  • If you have recently generated a QR Pass, wait until its 1-hour validity expires.

5. Pending Return Entry

  • If your QR was scanned while exiting the campus, you must use the same QR Pass when returning.
  • A new QR Pass cannot be generated until your return entry is recorded by the watchman.

6. Consistent Google Account

  • You must log in using the exact same Google account used to register and create your PIN.
  • Changing Google accounts will trigger identity verification security blocks and prevent pass generation.
Note: All exit and entry records are automatically stored in the system. Do not share your QR Pass or Security PIN with others. Contact the Administrator if you continue to face any issues.
